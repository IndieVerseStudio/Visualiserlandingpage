Figma Component-Level Prompts

Use these for generating individual components in Figma AI or similar tools.

### Before/After Slider Component

```
Design a before/after image comparison slider component. Dark card with rounded corners (12px radius). The image fills the card. A vertical divider line in white with a circular drag handle (white circle with left/right arrows) sits in the middle. Left side labeled "Before" and right side labeled "After" in small white text at the top corners. Below the image: a label in mint (#2affc0) text like "Complex Exterior". Card has a subtle 1px border in rgba(42, 255, 192, 0.2). Dark background (#2b2b2d).
```

### Feature/Step Card

```
Design a feature card component on a dark background (#2b2b2d). Card background is slightly lighter (#353538). Rounded corners (16px). Top: a large step number "01" in mint (#2affc0), semi-transparent (30% opacity), oversized. Below: title in white bold text "Upload Any Image". Below: description in gray (#a0a0a0) text. Bottom: a placeholder image area with rounded corners. Card has generous padding (32px). Subtle mint glow on hover state.
```

### CTA Button Pair

```
Design two side-by-side CTA buttons on a dark background (#2b2b2d).
Button 1: Solid fill with mint (#2affc0), dark text (#2b2b2d), bold rounded font, generous padding (16px 32px), rounded corners (8px). Label: "Try It On Your Building".
Button 2: Ghost/outline style, 2px mint border, mint text, same size and shape. Label: "Book a Demo".
Both buttons have subtle hover states — Button 1 gets slightly lighter, Button 2 fills with mint at 10% opacity.
```

### Deployment Option Card

```
Design a deployment option card. Dark background (#2b2b2d), card in #353538 with 1px border in rgba(42, 255, 192, 0.15). Rounded corners (16px). Top: a simple line icon in mint (#2affc0) — a cloud, code brackets, puzzle piece, or server icon. Middle: title in white bold "White-Label SDK". Bottom: one line of gray description text "Embed in your app or website, fully branded as yours." Padding 24px. On hover, the top border becomes a full mint-to-purple gradient line.
```

### Metric/Stat Card

```
Design a metrics card for social proof. Dark background (#2b2b2d). Three stat blocks in a row, separated by subtle vertical dividers (1px, rgba white 10%). Each block: a large number in mint (#2affc0) bold text (e.g., "10M+"), and a label below in small gray text (e.g., "Visualizations Served"). Centered text in each block. Clean, minimal, no card borders.
```

## PROMPT SET 4: Background / Decorative Elements

### Curved Line Background Pattern

```
Abstract decorative background pattern on dark charcoal (#2b2b2d). Two or three flowing, organic curved lines — one in mint/cyan (#2affc0), one in teal (#35d2b8), one in purple (#5455a3). Lines are thin (1-2px), smooth, and sweep across the canvas in large, gentle arcs. They should feel like orbital paths or flowing ribbons. Very subtle, not busy. Large negative space. No other elements. Use as a website background texture. 1920x1080.
```

### Gradient Accent Blob

```
A soft, blurred gradient blob on a dark charcoal background (#2b2b2d). The blob is an organic amorphous shape blending from mint (#2affc0) through teal (#35d2b8) to deep purple (#690094). Very soft edges, almost like a diffused light. Positioned in the top-right corner of the canvas, partially cropped. Subtle, atmospheric, used as a background accent for a dark-themed website. 1920x1080. Minimal, clean.
```

## Copy Reference — Key Messaging

Use these as headlines and body copy throughout the page:

**Headlines (pick and choose):**
- "See Paint on Any Building Before a Single Brushstroke"
- "The Paint Visualiser That Actually Works on Real Buildings"
- "Your Customers' Walls Aren't Simple. Your Visualiser Shouldn't Be Either."
- "Photorealistic Paint Visualization. Any Surface. Any Complexity."
- "The Most Accurate Paint Visualiser in the World"

**Subheadlines:**
- "The AI paint visualiser that handles complex exteriors, cluttered interiors, and everything your current tool can't."
- "Powered by cutting-edge AI that understands every paintable surface in an image — no matter how complex the scene."
- "Not a color overlay. A true preview of how paint will look — with accurate lighting, shadows, and texture."

**Key phrases to weave in:**
- "Upload any image — interior or exterior, simple or complex"
- "AI identifies every paintable surface automatically"
- "Photorealistic results, not flat color overlays"
- "Works on complex facades, ornate architecture, cluttered rooms"
- "Your brand colors, your customers' buildings"
- "Trusted by one of India's top 3 paint brands"
- "Web, mobile, API, or on-premise — however you need it"
- "Free 60-day pilot, 200 visualizations, no commitment"

**What NOT to say:**
- Do not use the words "masks", "segments", "segmentation", or "masking"
- Do not explain the technical pipeline (mask generation, etc.)
- Do not say "GenAI generates the painted image" — say the AI identifies/understands surfaces
- Do not name the Indian client
- Do not target Indian paint companies
- Keep it simple, visual, benefit-driven — executives, not engineers
